## What the video is

A bar-chart race of AI assistant **websites by monthly visits**, worldwide, December 2022 to September 2026, using **Similarweb's published estimates only** (Similarweb's own posts, reports and free website pages, plus reputable press quoting Similarweb). It is not market share and not users. One supplier, one measure.
