## Why
The channel's five public videos get almost no views. YouTube Studio (read by Cowork 10 Oct):
- Consoles: 980 impressions, 0.2% click-through.
- F1: 490 impressions, 3.1%.
- Browser Wars: 12 impressions in four days.

All five thumbnails use the same dark navy card with two bars and a gold headline. They do not look like a bar chart race.

The leading channel in this niche, Global Stats (334k subscribers), uses one layout on nearly every video, e.g. "Most Popular Web Browsers: Rise and Fall (1992–2025)", 1.2m views:
- left half: a real race frame with many bars and a logo, flag or face on each;
- right half: 2–4 huge words naming the subject, plus the years;
- a row of famous logos or a cut-out picture;
- a white or bright background.

Luke's own Market Marathon video in a similar layout got 672 views. We copy the layout convention, never their artwork, and never lower our evidence standard.
