IPU has a rich set of data on the number and percentage of women in parliament from 1945 to the current day. Since 1997, IPU has published a monthly ranking of the percentage of women in parliament . Since 2018, these rankings are now updated in Parline every month. Data on the age of parliamentarians was also incorporated into Parline at that time. | The technical architecture has also evolved over time, with the introduction of new features to view, export and reuse data from Parline.
