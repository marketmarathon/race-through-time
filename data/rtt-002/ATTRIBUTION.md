# Attribution — RTT-002 dataset

This dataset is derived from **English Wikipedia**, which is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

**Our derived dataset (everything in `data/rtt-002/`) is released under CC BY-SA 4.0.** Changes made: race winners, dates and links were extracted from the season-page tables, link targets resolved through Wikipedia redirects to stable page IDs, and cumulative win counts, career totals and the record progression were computed. No values were added from other sources.

Suggested credit line for video descriptions: *Race data derived from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), "Formula One season" and "Formula One World Championship" articles, 1950–2026, retrieved 28 September 2026.*

Formula 1, F1 and related marks belong to their owners; they are used descriptively only. No data was taken from formula1.com, and no Jolpica/Ergast data was used (DECISIONS.md DEC-003, DEC-008).
