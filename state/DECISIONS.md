- **DEC-012 · 28 Sep 2026 · Owner (D-04).** RTT-002 follows the official Formula 1 treatment. Evidence (formula1.com, read not scraped): the Indianapolis 500 counted towards the World Championship 1950–1960, and F1 treats those wins as World Championship Grand Prix wins — e.g. "Troy Ruttman … won just once, in 1952, to become the youngest World Championship Grand Prix winner" (F1's "one-win wonders" article, 4 Apr 2020); Sam Hanks's 1957 Indy win is listed among the oldest F1 Grand Prix winners (article of 18 Jun 2026). So the 11 Indy 500 wins stay in. Shared drives: one win to each credited driver, as in F1's race results. Tie order (whoever reached the total first ranks higher) is a display rule, not data; there is no official rule for it.

- **DEC-017 · 28 Sep 2026 · Claude working choice (IQ-04, step at event).** A count changes on the first frame of its race and holds until the next race; no in-between value is ever drawn. Only the bar's length glides (0.35 s, `glide.bar_sec`) and its row glides with C2-2's 0.55 s eased rank glide. Consequence kept from C2-2: a driver who jumps into the top ten from far below slides up from under the board and is fully faded for up to 22 frames (0.73 s) in the 1984–89 slice, so their label appears then, correct from its first visible frame. Event start times are whole frames, so tests can land on them exactly.

The player refuses any multiplier outside 0.8–1.4. Full RTT-002 run = 16,062 frames at 30 fps (8 min 55 s including 3 s intro and 8 s closing card). IQ-05 sets the real pace.
