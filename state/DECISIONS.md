- **DEC-023 · 28 Sep 2026 · Claude working choice (IQ-05, colours).** Each driver gets one colour for the whole RTT-002 video, assigned by `rtt_timeline.js` `assignColours()` over the whole race file (never the passage window), so a pilot and the full video agree. "On screen together" = the top ten after a race plus the top ten after each of the 3 races before it (`colour_rule.fade_races`), because a row that drops out takes up to about 23 frames to fade. Such drivers never share a colour, and their colours must also be at least CIEDE2000 18 apart as drawn (`colour_rule.min_delta_e`), so near-copies do not count as "different". Drivers are coloured in the order they first reach the top ten, each taking the first palette colour that fits; the player refuses to render if the palette runs out, or if the pace is so fast that 3 races cannot cover the fade. Result: 40 drivers ever reach the top ten and at most 12 can be on screen together, so 12 colours are needed. IQ-04's 12 were not clearly distinct once drawn (closest pair CIEDE2000 9.6: three purples, two greens, two blues), and no bigger palette can help the moment when 12 are on screen, so the palette was replaced by 12 colours chosen from a grid of candidates to be as far apart as possible (closest pair CIEDE2000 18.5, about twice IQ-04's) while every bar keeps white-name contrast of at least 3.2:1 (IQ-04's weakest was 3.21). C2-2's first colour `#2E86DE` was kept; vivid colours are listed first so the earthier last four (mustard, forest green, brown, khaki) are used only when needed. The formula was checked against the published CIEDE2000 reference pairs. No team or nationality colours.
