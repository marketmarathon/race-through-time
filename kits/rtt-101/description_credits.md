- What the bars show: each club's cumulative net spend on reported transfer fees (fees paid minus fees received) in its Premier League seasons, from the 1992–93 season to the close of the summer 2026 window (1 September 2026, 11pm BST). Fees are nominal pounds, never adjusted for inflation. Only fees confirmed at their source are counted (club and league statements, contemporary press, and Soccerbase career tables where they match the transfer).

- The panel adds up every club's figure: all Premier League clubs' net spend with clubs outside the Premier League (deals between two PL clubs cancel out).
