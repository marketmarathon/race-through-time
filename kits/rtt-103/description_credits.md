## Video description (draft)
What the bars show: each company's capital spending (cash spent on buildings and equipment, as each company reports it) over the previous 12 months, in US dollars, from its own filings. Amazon is shown net of proceeds and incentives; Tencent's figure is measured differently (additions, including some intangible assets); Alibaba's figures before April 2016 include some licensed content and other intangible assets, and its figures for mid-2016 to 2017 are not comparable, so it is not shown then. Chinese companies' figures are converted at the average Federal Reserve H.10 exchange rate for each quarter. The bars are total capital spending, not AI-only spending: no company reports AI spending separately.

2026 plans: Alphabet, Microsoft, Meta and CoreWeave from their results calls and releases; Amazon and Oracle as reported by Reuters; Alibaba: Citi estimate; Tencent and Baidu: actual 12 months to June 2026 (Tencent said it aims to boost capital spending in 2026, Reuters, 18 Mar 2026; Baidu gives no outlook). ByteDance: press report from unnamed sources (South China Morning Post, 9 May 2026), not company guidance.

Look-ahead 2027–2030: Race Through Time estimates, not forecasts by the companies: each company's 2026 figure grown at FactSet consensus growth rates (published by Morgan Stanley Investment Management, 17 Sep 2026); Alibaba uses Citi's estimates to March 2029. 2030 is the least reliable year. Oracle's 2027–2028 estimates are probably high. Both rating agencies expect CoreWeave's spending to dip in 2027 (Fitch, 17 Jul 2026; S&P Global Ratings, 9 Apr 2026).

When does it peak? BCG (12 Jun 2026), FactSet consensus (31 Aug 2026) and Allianz Research (28 Sep 2026, AI capital spending only) disagree.

Estimate: iCapital (30 Apr 2026, footnoting S&P Capital IQ) estimates that 70–75% of Amazon's, Microsoft's, Alphabet's and Meta's spending is now tied directly to AI infrastructure.
